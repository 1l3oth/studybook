12-minute Korean speech practice

  1. 1. 0–2 min · See four pictures

    Think of four scenes: new hobby → food from home and saving money → cooking 차 스빼이 → chilies and salty food. Use the last line in your submitted copy to finish.

  2. 2. 2–5 min · Say the first lines

    Say “제 새로운 취미는 요리입니다.” Then cover the line and say it again. Do the same with the first line of each scene in your submitted copy.

  3. 3. 5–9 min · Tell the whole story

    Hide the script and say it aloud. If you get stuck, look at just the next line, say it, then keep going.

  4. 4. 9–12 min · Fix one spot

    Say the hardest line with the line right before it. Then start from the beginning and give one full run aloud.
