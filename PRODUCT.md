# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

Installable as a PWA on Rex's phone (home-screen app, standalone window). Online only: no offline
caching of units was requested (2026-11-05).

## Users

Rex (Daraboth Vong), a Cambodian software developer working in Seoul, learning Korean at about TOPIK
1 to 2. He is the main user. He opens a unit on his phone in short sessions (commute, breaks) to
memorise spoken material: a presentation script, or the phrases for a real situation such as visiting
a coworker's funeral. The site is public on GitHub Pages, but other learners are not the target.

## Product Purpose

A notebook of Korean study units. Each unit turns one topic into memory hooks: a picture per idea,
the Korean line, its English meaning, native-voice audio, and a hide-to-recall toggle, plus the
questions someone may ask him, the topic words, and the full text. Success: he can recall and use the
lines without reading them.

## Positioning

Every unit has the same four parts (picture cards, Q&A that leads with traps and gaps, vocab with
two-way hide, full printable text) and the same audio engine, so the habit carries from unit to unit.
Units are generated by an AI skill (`/teach-me`, the `studybook` skill), and `feed.xml` carries each
unit's full text so an AI can read the notebook without running it.

## Operating Context

- Phone first, portrait, one hand; also opened on a desktop browser at work.
- He is often at his desk at work, so recall happens silently: never require speaking aloud.
- Short, spaced sessions; units are revisited many times.
- Hosted at `https://1l3oth.github.io/studybook/` (a sub-path, not a domain root).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages: plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no build tooling beyond the Python
  standard-library builders (`tools/render_unit.py` from each `unit.json`, `build_stations.py`, `build_feed.py`).
- Units: `/<unit-slug>/index.html` with `img/` and `audio/` beside it (web build).
- Korean and English text; Korean needs a proper Korean face and `word-break: keep-all`.
- Audio: per-line MP3 clips, native Korean voice.

## Brand Commitments

- Unit pictures are flat 2D drawings of objects (Rex, 2026-11-05), not generated scenes or photos.
- Name: 다라봇 스터디북 (Studybook).

## Evidence on Hand

- `korean-summer-speech/`: 32 speech lines, 29 Q&A, 89 words, 163 audio clips, 11 images.
- `korean-funeral-visit/`: new unit in progress (condolence visit at work).
- `feed.xml`: full text of each unit.

## Product Principles

1. Recall before reveal: hiding is the core action, never an afterthought.
2. One structure for every unit, so nothing has to be relearned.
3. Hear it in a native voice, one tap away from every line.
4. Short sessions win: a unit must be useful in two minutes on a phone.
5. Focus first (Rex, 2026-11-05): the page helps him concentrate and learn; nothing on screen competes
   with the line he is recalling. Fully mobile responsive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable Korean at phone sizes, light and dark themes, large tap targets for one-handed use.
