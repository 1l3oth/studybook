Visiting a funeral hall · When a coworker's family member dies

한눈에Hannune · The big picture

A Korean funeral usually lasts 3 days in a hospital funeral hall. Coworkers visit to share the family's grief. You do not need many words.

  1. 삼일장SamiljangThe funeral lasts 3 days
  2. 검은 옷Geomeun otDark clothes, nothing bright
  3. 흰 봉투Hin bongtuMoney helps the family pay
  4. 흰 국화Hin gukwaA white flower says respect
  5. 두 번 · 한 번Du beon · han beonBow twice to the deceased, once to the family
  6. 식사SiksaThe family feeds every guest

노선도 · 조문 순서Noseondo · jomun sunseo

One visit, in order: hear the news, get ready, pay respects, comfort the family, share the meal.

Lyric: Korean in English letters, so you can read it out loud. eo sounds like the u in up, eu like the oo in good, ae like the e in bed.

05조문Jomun · At the altar국화Gukwa

06위로Wiro · Comfort상주Sangju

상주에게 한 번 절하고 '얼마나 마음이 아프세요' 하고 조용히 말해요.Sangjuege han beon jeolhago 'eolmana maeumi apeuseyo' hago joyonghi malhaeyo.

I bow once to the chief mourner and quietly say, 'You must be heartbroken.'

함정 · 헷갈리는 것Hamjeong · hetgallineun geot

Q. 꼭 절해야 돼요?Kkok jeolhaeya dwaeyo?

Q. Do I have to bow?

A. 아니요. 종교 때문에 어려우면 묵념만 해도 돼요.Aniyo. Jonggyo ttaemune eoryeoumyeon mungnyeomman haedo dwaeyo.

A. No. If bowing is hard for religious reasons, a silent prayer is fine.

Q. 부의금은 얼마 내요?Buigeumeun eolma naeyo?

Q. How much condolence money do I give?

A. 보통 오만 원이나 십만 원을 내요. 팀 사람들하고 맞춰도 돼요.Botong oman wonina simman woneul naeyo. Tim saramdeulhago matchwodo dwaeyo.

A. Usually 50,000 or 100,000 won. You can match the amount with your team.
